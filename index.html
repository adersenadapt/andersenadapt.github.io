ABOUT

My career has taken a few turns.
The red thread hasn’t.

I started with languages and international communication, moved into digital marketing and SEO, ended up managing international markets and people, and then went increasingly deep into translation, localisation technology, project management and operations.

These days, I work in business development — which, in my case, mostly means taking all of those things and figuring out how they should fit together.

It looks slightly chaotic on LinkedIn.
There is a system to it. Promise.

I’ve never been particularly good at leaving inefficient processes alone. Give me a spreadsheet nobody understands, a localisation workflow held together by hope, or a CAT tool behaving strangely and I’ll probably want to fix it.
